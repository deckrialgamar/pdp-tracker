# PDP Tracker

Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law, UU 27/2022, read against its implementing regulation, PP 33/2026, article by article. Live at https://pdp-tracker-zeta.vercel.app, with the wiki at https://pdp-tracker-zeta.vercel.app/wiki/.

- `index.html` is the tracker: both instruments article by article, the draft against the final text, the Practitioner Notes and the executive summary.
- `wiki/index.html` is the UU PDP wiki: one page for each of the Law's 76 articles, with the text, the official elucidation, the implementing rules, commentary, and the European decisions and guidance on the matching GDPR provisions.
- `wiki/gdpr-map.html` shows the GDPR's 99 articles as a network, with the Court's judgments, EDPB documents and national decisions placed on it.
- `sources/` holds the official copies of UU 27/2022, PP 33/2026 and three Constitutional Court decisions that the pages cite.

This repository holds the built pages only. They are generated in a private build and copied here, and a push to `main` redeploys the site. See `about.html` for who wrote it and how it was checked.
